About Porto

Porto's riverside Ribeira district is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the port wine cellars sit across the river in Vila Nova de Gaia. Upriver, the Douro valley is one of the world's oldest wine regions.

The drive takes about three hours. Many travellers break the journey in Coimbra, Aveiro or Fátima.

Worth seeing

  • Ribeira and the Douro riverfront
  • Port wine cellars in Gaia
  • Douro valley
  • Coimbra and Aveiro on the way

How to go

  • One-way or return transfer, from the airport, your hotel, the cruise port or any address.
  • Day trip with the driver waiting and moving you between stops.
  • Stop in Coimbra, Aveiro or Fátima on the way.
