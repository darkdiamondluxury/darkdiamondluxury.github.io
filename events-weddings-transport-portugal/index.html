Events we work with

  • Large events. International conferences and fairs, such as Web Summit.
  • Corporate events. Meetings, conventions, launches and incentive trips.
  • Professional sport. Teams, organisations and guests at professional sporting events.
  • Golf. Tournaments and golf trips, with room for luggage.
  • Weddings. Couples and guests, from the ceremony to the venue and back.

How we work

  • A single point of contact for the whole operation.
  • Times agreed with the organisers.
  • Vehicles for groups: V-Class, Sprinter and a 19-seat minibus.
  • As many vehicles as the number of guests requires.
  • Drivers who speak Portuguese, English, Spanish and French.

How it works

  1. Send the event detailsDates, venues and expected number of guests.
  2. Proposal and priceSent in writing, with the vehicles and the times.
  3. Planning with the organisersPick-ups, routes and times agreed in advance.
  4. On the dayDirect coordination with the drivers, so any change is handled straight away.

Preparing an event?
