What we do at the port

  • Port and airport. Transfers on embarkation and disembarkation day.
  • Port and hotel. For stays before or after the cruise.
  • Private shore excursions. Lisbon, Sintra, Cascais or Fátima, with a driver who plans the return around the all-aboard time.
  • Groups. V-Class, Sprinter and a 19-seat minibus, with room for luggage.

Lisbon's cruise terminals

Most ships dock at the Lisbon Cruise Terminal, next to Santa Apolónia and Alfama, close to the city centre and around 20 minutes from the airport.

Some ships use the Alcântara or Rocha Conde de Óbidos terminals, further west. Tell us the ship's name and we will meet you at the right place.

Back on board on time

On shore excursions we plan the day backwards from the all-aboard time, with a margin for traffic. You keep the driver's number and the driver keeps yours.

Docking in Lisbon?

Tell us the ship, the date and what you would like to do. We reply with a plan and a price.
