About Cascais

Cascais grew from a fishing village into a resort town, with a marina, beaches and a walkable old centre. The road along the Estoril coast is one of the most scenic in the Lisbon area.

From Cascais it is a short drive to Boca do Inferno and Cabo da Roca, the westernmost point of mainland Europe.

Worth seeing

  • Cascais old town and marina
  • Boca do Inferno
  • Cabo da Roca
  • Estoril and its casino
