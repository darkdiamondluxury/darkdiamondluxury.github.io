Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and your rights.

Last updated: October 2026.

Who is responsible for your data

Artur Danilo, Unipessoal Lda (Dark Diamond Luxury), VAT no. 515565458, Av. Vergílio Ferreira, Lote 764, 6.º Esq., 1950-339 Lisboa, Portugal. Contact: darkdiamondluxury@gmail.com.

This website

  • It does not use cookies.
  • When you send the booking form, we store the request (service, route, date, time, flight, passengers, luggage, name, phone, email and notes) in our service management system (Supabase), only to reply to you and organise the service. If you do not book, we delete the request.
  • The form also opens WhatsApp with the message ready. What you send there is processed by WhatsApp (Meta) and by us.
  • We count visits anonymously with GoatCounter, without cookies and without identifying visitors.

When you book a service

We process your name, contact details, flight details, pick-up and drop-off addresses, number of passengers and any notes you give us, to provide the service (performance of a contract) and to meet tax and accounting obligations. The necessary details are shared with the driver who carries out the service. If the booking comes from an agency or partner, we receive these details from them.

We keep the data for as long as needed for the service and then for the period required by law for tax purposes.

Internal app (Despacho)

Our team and drivers use an internal app to organise services. Drivers' location is recorded only when they update the status of a service, and is visible only to management. Agencies and partners never have access to location data.

Your rights

You can request access to, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to darkdiamondluxury@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD (www.cnpd.pt).
